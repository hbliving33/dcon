Engagement

Work with us the way that fits.

One job, ongoing support or a senior IT leader part time. Start with one, add another later.

Which one fits?

I need…

Option 01

Projects

A defined job with a clear finish line.

A fixed scope and a price agreed before work starts. When it is done, it is documented and handed back to you.

What's included

  • Written scope, timeline and fixed price
  • One senior lead from start to finish
  • Documentation and handoff when the job is done

Typical examples

  • Office or headquarters moves
  • Microsoft 365 migrations
  • Network or Wi-Fi refreshes
  • Security assessments
  • Access control installs
Best for: One-time needs with a clear goal
Option 02

Managed support

We keep it running, every month.

A monthly agreement that covers the day-to-day: users, devices, updates, vendors and a real person when something breaks.

What's included

  • Monitoring and patching
  • User onboarding and offboarding
  • Vendor and license management
  • Help when something breaks
  • A regular review of what changed and what is next

Typical examples

  • Companies without in-house IT
  • Teams whose IT person left
  • Offices that need backup for a small IT staff
Best for: Teams without in-house IT
Option 03

Fractional IT leadership

A senior IT leader, part time.

The judgment of an IT director for a set number of days a month, without a full-time salary.

What's included

  • Technology roadmap and budget
  • Vendor contract review and negotiation
  • Audit, compliance and risk ownership
  • Leadership and board reporting
  • Hiring and managing outside providers

Typical examples

  • Growing companies
  • Businesses facing an audit or acquisition
  • Leadership teams that need an IT voice at the table
Best for: Companies not ready for a full-time IT director

How an engagement starts

  1. Send us a message

    Tell us what's going on through the contact form. We reply within one business day.

  2. Get a written proposal

    A clear scope, timeline and price, in plain English. No surprises later.

  3. Kick off

    We get to work, keep you posted, and document everything as we go.

Many clients combine them: a project to fix the foundation, then managed support to keep it that way.
