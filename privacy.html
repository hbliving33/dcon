Privacy

Privacy policy.

Effective October 16, 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by D Consulting. This policy explains what information we collect through the site, how we use it and who we share it with.

Information we collect

When you send us a message through the contact form, we collect the information you enter: your name, company, email address, phone number (optional), what you need help with, your company size and your message.

The IT health check on our Why Us page runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us unless you choose to include your score in a message.

Like any website, the services that host and deliver this site may receive standard technical information when a page loads, such as your IP address and browser type.

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

We use the information you send us to reply to you and to provide the services you ask about. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that run this site and deliver your message:

  • FormSubmit delivers contact form messages to our email. Its documentation says submissions are kept for 30 days.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts used on this site.
  • Our email provider receives and stores the messages you send us.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Do Not Track

We do not track visitors over time or across other websites. Because of that, the site does not change its behavior in response to browser Do Not Track signals.

Reviewing or changing your information

To review, correct or delete information you've sent us, send us a request through our contact form.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Send them through our contact form.
